# Changelog
All notable changes to the RPG Adventure game.

## [Unreleased]
### Added
- **New Boss Monsters**: Fire Drake, Ice Dragon, Shadow Knight, Dark Lord (ultimate boss)
  - Each has unique health pools, attack patterns, and special abilities
  - Fire Drake: 450 HP, Fire Breath ability
  - Ice Dragon: 420 HP, Ice Nova ability  
  - Shadow Knight: 350 HP, Shadow Step ability
  - Dark Lord: 800 HP, Dark Presence (final boss)
- **New Quests**: Main storyline quests for each boss plus optional side challenges
- **Boss Drop Items**:
  - Fire Crystal (+5 Magic)
  - Ice Shard (+5 Magic)  
  - Shadow Shard (+3 Strength, life drain)
  - Dark Lord's Heart (+15 Strength legendary weapon)
  - Fire Elixir and Frost Brew consumables
- **New Biome Regions**:
  - Volcano region (Fire Drake arena) with lava terrain
  - Frozen Peaks (Ice Dragon arena) with snow terrain
  - Dark Caverns (Shadow Knight arena) with shadow wisps
  - Abyss Arena (Dark Lord arena) with dark mist terrain
- **Spawn System**: Weighted random spawns for boss encounters
  - Regular bosses: 5-7% spawn rate
  - Ultra-rare Dark Lord: 5% overall chance
- **Visual Enhancements**:
  - New terrain renders for lava, snow, shadow, and abyss biomes
  - Enhanced world generation with boss-specific regions
- **Bug Fix**: Removed blocking HTML report from package.json test scripts
